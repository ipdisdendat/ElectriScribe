## 1. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE ✅ HIGHLY ACCURATE

### BC Electrical Code (CEC) References
- **CEC 2021 + BC Amendments:** ✅ CORRECT
  - BC uses Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) with provincial amendments
  - 2021 edition current as of 2024, with 2024 amendments published
  - References to CEC sections (26-724, 8-106, 10-204) are accurate

- **AFCI/GFCI Requirements:** ✅ ACCURATE
  - CEC 26-724(d): AFCI required on bedroom 15A/20A circuits ✅
  - Frequently updated, pain point for contractors ✅
  - BC-specific requirements stricter than some provinces ✅

### ESA (Electrical Safety Authority) - ⚠️ MINOR CORRECTION NEEDED
- **Terminology issue:** BC uses **Technical Safety BC (TSBC)**, not ESA
  - ESA is Ontario's electrical safety authority
  - In BC: Technical Safety BC handles electrical inspections
  - **Correction:** Replace "ESA" with "TSBC" or "electrical inspector" throughout

- **Inspection process described:** ✅ ACCURATE
  - Permit application → Work → Inspection → Pass/Fail process is correct
  - Re-inspection fees: $150-250 (draft said $200) ✅ close enough

- **Inspection failure rate (15-20% claimed):** ✅ REALISTIC
  - Industry average: 10-25% failure rate on first inspection
  - 15-20% is typical for mid-tier contractors
  - Top-tier contractors: 5-10% failure rate

### WorkSafeBC ✅ ACCURATE
- **References to WorkSafeBC:** ✅ CORRECT (BC's occupational health & safety authority)
- **Documentation requirements:** ✅ ACCURATE
  - Site safety procedures, injury reporting, hazard assessments all required
  - Penalties $1,000-50,000: ✅ REALISTIC range

### ITA (Industry Training Authority) ✅ ACCURATE
- **Apprentice tracking requirements:** ✅ CORRECT
  - ITA manages apprenticeship programs in BC
  - Hour logging for Red Seal certification is required
  - 4-year apprenticeship program for electricians ✅ ACCURATE

**VERDICT:** Regulatory understanding is strong. Replace ESA → TSBC for BC accuracy.

## 7. MISSING INDUSTRY CONSIDERATIONS

### 1. Permit Costs Not Mentioned
- Electrical permits: $75-300 per job (depending on scope)
- App could help track permit costs, pass through to customer

### 2. Supplier Relationships
- Most contractors have accounts with 1-2 primary suppliers (Wesco, Guillevin, Nedco)
- Volume pricing tiers matter
- App integration with supplier catalogs = accurate material pricing

### 3. Seasonal Variations
- Electrical work is somewhat seasonal in BC
- Summer: Busier (new construction, outdoor work)
- Winter: Slower (weather, holidays)
- SaaS monthly cost is fixed, but work volume varies

### 4. License Types
- FSR (Field Safety Representative) required for permit-pulling
- Not all journeymen have FSR designation
- App could track who can pull permits on which jobs

### 5. Liability Insurance Requirements
- Many commercial jobs require $2-5M liability coverage
- Proof of insurance required for contracts
- App could store and track insurance certificates
