## PILOT PROGRAM DESIGN VALIDATION

### Pilot Pricing Confusion

**GTM Plan States:**
- "90-day pilot at 50% discount ($20/user/month)"
- Also states: "30-day free trial, no credit card"

**Question:** Which is it? Paid pilot or free trial?

**Recommendation:** **CLARIFY PILOT vs TRIAL**

**Option A: Free 30-Day Trial (Self-Serve)**
- **For:** Self-serve signups (website, app store)
- **Length:** 30 days (industry standard)
- **No payment:** No credit card required
- **Support:** Email/chat support, no sales calls
- **Conversion:** 20-40% (typical self-serve SaaS)

**Option B: 90-Day Paid Pilot (Sales-Assisted)**
- **For:** Direct sales prospects (lighthouse, early adopters)
- **Length:** 90 days (matches behavior change timeline)
- **Payment:** 50% discount = $20/user/month × 3 months = $600 for 10 users
- **Support:** Weekly check-ins, hands-on onboarding, training workshop
- **Conversion:** 75-80% (sales-assisted, skin in the game)

**Why Paid Pilot is Better for Direct Sales:**
- ✅ **Commitment:** $600 payment proves owner is serious (filters tire-kickers)
- ✅ **Ownership:** Electricians know company paid for it (more likely to try)
- ✅ **Conversion:** Sunk cost fallacy works in your favor (harder to walk away)
- ✅ **Respect:** Sales team invests 25-30 hours, pilot payment compensates time

**Why Free Trial is Better for Self-Serve:**
- ✅ **Low friction:** Anyone can try, no purchase approval
- ✅ **Volume:** Can run 20-30 trials simultaneously (vs 5 managed pilots)
- ❌ **Conversion:** Lower (20-40% vs 75%) but volume compensates

**Recommended Model:**
- **Direct Sales:** 90-day paid pilot at $20/user/month (sales-assisted)
- **Self-Serve:** 30-day free trial (no sales involvement)
- **Channel Partners:** 60-day free trial (partners don't want to collect payment)

**3. Prove Offline Mode:**
- **Live demo:** Put phone in airplane mode, document panel, show it works
- **Case study:** "Electrician documented panel in basement with no signal, synced when back in truck"

**4. Differentiate from Competitors:**
- **Unique:** "Only app with BC electrical code validation built-in"

## CRITICAL GAPS IN SALES PROCESS

### Gap 1: No Multi-Threading Strategy

**Current Plan:** Sell to owner/manager only

**Risk:** Owner gets busy, stops engaging → Pilot fails even if electricians love it

**Multi-Threading Strategy (Recommended):**

**1. Owner (Economic Buyer)**
- **Role:** Signs contract, pays bill
- **Engagement:** Results review, ROI proof
- **Touchpoint:** Monthly check-in (not weekly)

**2. Champion Electrician (Technical Buyer)**
- **Role:** Advocates to team, provides product feedback
- **Engagement:** Weekly check-ins, feature requests
- **Touchpoint:** Weekly (more frequent than owner)

**3. Office Admin (User)**
- **Role:** Uses QuickBooks integration, manages customer data
- **Engagement:** Setup support, reporting
- **Touchpoint:** As-needed support

**Why This Matters:**
- **If owner ghosts you:** Champion keeps pilot alive
- **If champion leaves company:** Owner relationship preserves deal
- **Multiple advocates:** Harder for company to walk away

**Implementation:**
- **Discovery call:** Ask "Who else should be involved in this evaluation?"
- **Onboarding:** Train champion + owner (not just champion)
- **Check-ins:** Alternate weeks - Week 1 champion, Week 2 owner, Week 3 champion, Week 4 owner

---

### Gap 2: Champion Identification Timing

**Current Plan:** Week 3-4 of pilot (trial setup phase)

**Problem:** Too late - champion should be identified **before demo**

**Why:**
- **Demo customization:** Tailor demo to champion's pain points
- **Trial setup:** Champion should be on onboarding call (not discovered after setup)
- **Advocacy:** Champion should convince owner to start trial (bottom-up + top-down)

**Recommended Timeline:**

**Discovery Call (Week 1):**
- **Ask:** "Who on your team is most comfortable with new technology?"
- **Ask:** "If we run a pilot, who would be the best person to test it first?"
- **Validate:** Owner confirms champion (or you identify red flag: "No one on my team likes tech")

**Demo (Week 2):**
- **Invite:** Owner + champion on demo call
- **Address:** Champion's specific pain points (not just owner's business case)
- **Close:** "Champion, would you be willing to test this for 90 days?"

**Trial Setup (Week 3):**
- **Champion leads:** Champion trains other electricians (peer-to-peer more effective)
- **Ownership:** Champion feels responsible for pilot success

---

### Gap 3: Failed Pilot Rescue Plan

**Current Plan:** Scenario C mentions 90-day extension at 50% discount

**Gap:** What if pilot is clearly failing at Week 8?

**Recommended "Rescue Protocol":**

**Week 8 Health Check:**
- **Red flags:**
  - <50% electrician adoption (less than 5 out of 10 using weekly)
  - <70% parse confidence (AI extraction not accurate enough)
  - Owner hasn't logged into dashboard in 3 weeks
  - Champion says "team is complaining, this is harder than paper"

**If 2+ Red Flags:**

**Option A: Diagnose & Extend**
- **Action:** 45-min diagnostic call with owner + champion
- **Ask:** "What's blocking adoption?" (common answers: offline mode doesn't work, typing is too slow, QuickBooks sync broken)
- **Fix:** Address blockers (ship voice input, fix sync issue, prove offline mode)
- **Extend:** 60-day extension (same price, gives time to fix)
- **Go/No-Go:** If no improvement in 30 days, end pilot (don't drag out failure)

**Option B: Pivot to Power User Model**
- **Action:** "Looks like full team adoption is tough. What if we focus on 3-4 power users (estimators, project managers)?"
- **Pricing:** Reduce to 4 users × $40 = $160/month (vs $400 for 10 users)
- **Value:** Still generates ROI (quotes, compliance checks) without forcing reluctant electricians

**Option C: Graceful Exit**
- **Action:** "This isn't working for your team. Let's pause the pilot."
- **Offer:** "If you want to try again in 6 months (when we ship voice input feature), let me know."
- **Preserve:** Relationship for future (don't burn bridge)

**Why This Matters:**
- **5% of pilots will fail** (per GTM plan Scenario C)
- **Without rescue protocol:** 5% loss
- **With rescue protocol:** 50% of failing pilots salvage to power user model or future re-engagement
