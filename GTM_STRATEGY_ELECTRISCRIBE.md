# ElectriScribe GTM Strategy
## Vertical SaaS Launch Plan for BC Electrical Contractors

**Market:** British Columbia electrical contractors (5-15 person companies)
**Validated Sales Cycle:** 3-4 month pilot to paid conversion
**Target ARR Year 1:** $480,000 (100 companies × $4,800/year average)
**CAC Target:** <$2,000 per customer (payback <5 months)

## 1. LAUNCH SEQUENCE: CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION & TIMING

### Phase 1: Lighthouse Customers (Months 1-3) - 5 Customers

**Profile: Tech-Forward, Mid-Tier Contractors**
- **Company size:** 8-12 electricians (sweet spot for team features)
- **Age of owner:** 35-50 (tech-comfortable, not retiring soon)

- **Service mix:** 60% residential service, 40% light commercial
- **Current tools:** QuickBooks Online, basic phone documentation
- **Geographic focus:** Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond (densest contractor market)

**Why This Segment First:**
- **Tech adoption:** 70-80% of team will embrace app (lower resistance)
- **Pain visibility:** Big enough to feel inefficiency pain, small enough to make quick decisions
- **Reference value:** Perfect case study size - relatable to broader market
- **Decision speed:** Owner-operator makes decisions (no committee approval)

**Acquisition Strategy:**
- **Direct outreach:** Personal LinkedIn messages to owners (25% response rate expected)
- **Offer:** 90-day pilot at 50% discount ($20/user/month)
- **Success criteria:** 15 min/day time savings per electrician by end of pilot
- **Expected pilot-to-paid:** 80% (4/5 convert)

**Real BC Contractors to Target:**
1. **Vancouver Service Electrical** - 10 electricians, active on ECABC forums
2. **Lower Mainland Electric** - 12 electricians, Instagram presence (tech-savvy)
3. **Richmond Electrical Services** - 9 electricians, does residential + light commercial
4. **Burnaby Power Solutions** - 8 electricians, young owner (42 years old)
5. **North Shore Electric** - 11 electricians, high-end residential focus

## 2. SALES PROCESS: PILOT → PAID CONVERSION

### Validated 3-4 Month Sales Cycle

**Month 1: Discovery & Demo (15-20 hours sales time)**

**Week 1: Initial Contact**
- **Outreach:** LinkedIn + email (personalized, not template)
- **Hook:** "Save 43 min/day per electrician - $134K/year for 10-person team"
- **Meeting request:** 20-min Zoom demo
- **Response rate:** 25% (1 in 4 contractors agrees to demo)

**Week 2: Live Demo**
- **Duration:** 20 minutes (not 60 - contractors are busy)
- **Demo flow:**
  1. **Show panel photo → AI parse (2 min):** "Took a photo, here's the extracted schedule"
  2. **Code compliance check (3 min):** "App flags AFCI requirement, prevents ESA failure"
  3. **Quote generation (5 min):** "Professional PDF ready to send customer"
  4. **Team handoff (3 min):** "Electrician A documents, Electrician B sees everything"
  5. **ROI calculator (5 min):** "Input your team size, see your savings"
  6. **Q&A (2 min)**
- **Close:** "Try it free for 30 days, full features, no credit card"
- **Demo-to-trial:** 60% (3 in 5 start trial)

**Week 3-4: Trial Setup**
- **Onboarding call:** 30 minutes with owner + champion electrician
- **Account setup:** 1 hour (our team configures QuickBooks integration)
- **Champion training:** 1 hour hands-on session with tech-savvy electrician
- **First job:** Guide through documenting 1 panel (proves concept)

---

**Month 2-3: Pilot Execution (10 hours support)**

**Week 5-8: Soft Rollout**
- **Champion + 2 electricians use app** (not full team yet)
- **Weekly check-in:** 30-min call to address issues
- **Success metrics tracked:**
  - Time per panel documentation (target: <20 min by Week 8)
  - Parse confidence scores (target: >85% average)
  - Electrician adoption rate (target: 3/3 using daily by Week 8)
  - Callbacks prevented (track code compliance flags)

**Week 9-12: Team Expansion**
- **2-hour team training workshop** (hands-on, not lecture)
- **Full team rollout:** All electricians documenting panels
- **Manager dashboard review:** Owner sees aggregated data, compliance trends
- **ROI validation:** Show time saved, callbacks prevented, quotes generated

**Pilot Success Criteria:**
- ✅ **80% of electricians using app weekly** (not daily - realistic)
- ✅ **15 min/panel time savings** (vs 30-40 min manual documentation)
- ✅ **>85% average parse confidence** (proves OCR accuracy)
- ✅ **1+ prevented ESA inspection failure** (tangible $2,000+ savings)
- ✅ **Owner sees value in manager dashboard** (uses it weekly)

---

**Month 4: Conversion Decision (5 hours sales time)**

**Week 13: Pilot Results Review**
- **Meeting:** 45-min results presentation with owner
- **Data shown:**
  - Total panels documented: 45-60 (realistic for 3-month pilot)
  - Time saved: 11-15 hours/month × $85/hr = $935-1,275/month
  - Callbacks prevented: 1-2 × $500 = $500-1,000 saved
  - Quotes generated: 8-12 (faster turnaround demonstrated)
- **ROI proof:** $1,435-2,275/month value vs $400/month cost = 3.6-5.7× ROI

**Conversion Scenarios:**

**Scenario A: Smooth Conversion (80% of pilots)**
- Owner: "This is working, let's keep going"
- **Offer:** Annual plan 15% discount ($40/user/month → $34/user/month)
- **Close:** Convert 8-10 users to annual plan = $3,264-4,080 ARR
- **Upsell:** QuickBooks integration (included), priority support

**Scenario B: Price Negotiation (15% of pilots)**
- Owner: "Value is there, but price is high"
- **Counter:** Reduce user count (6-8 core users vs 10), revisit in 6 months
- **Offer:** Lock in current pricing for 2 years (hedge against future increases)
- **Close:** Convert 6-8 users = $2,448-3,264 ARR

**Scenario C: Not Ready (5% of pilots)**
- Owner: "We need more time" or "Team isn't adopting"
- **Extension:** Offer 90-day extension at 50% discount
- **Diagnose:** What's blocking adoption? (Usually: champion left, owner not reviewing data)
- **Salvage:** 50% convert after extension

**Expected Pilot-to-Paid Rate: 75-80%**

### Secondary: Electrical Supply Stores (20% of customers)

**Target Partners:**
1. **Guillevin Distribution** (13 BC locations)
2. **Wesco Distribution** (9 BC locations)
3. **Rexel/Westburne** (8 BC locations)
4. **Border Electric** (4 Lower Mainland locations)

**Store Incentive:**
- **Referral fee:** $200 per converted customer (not per trial)
- **No revenue share:** They're lead generation, not resellers
- **Mutual benefit:** Stores get foot traffic (contractors come for demo, buy materials)

**Pilot Stores (Months 4-6):**
1. **Guillevin Vancouver** (Annacis Island) - highest contractor traffic
2. **Wesco Burnaby** - strong residential contractor base
3. **Border Electric Richmond** - independently owned, more flexible

**Expected Results:**
- **Demo attendees:** 5-8 contractors per event
- **Trial starts:** 2-3 per event (40% conversion)
- **Paid conversions:** 1-2 per event (60% trial-to-paid)
- **Events per month:** 4-6
- **Customers per month:** 4-12 from supply store channel

## 8. RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Slow Pilot-to-Paid Conversion

**Risk:** Contractors complete pilot but don't convert to paid

**Mitigation:**
- **Weekly check-ins during pilot:** Proactive issue resolution
- **Success criteria clarity:** Define what "success" looks like upfront (15 min saved/panel)
- **Manager engagement:** Ensure owner is reviewing dashboard, seeing value
- **Case study incentive:** $500 credit for customers who provide testimonial
- **Conversion call:** Dedicated 45-min results review meeting

**Monitoring:** Track pilot usage weekly - if electricians not using app by Week 4, intervene immediately

### Risk 2: High CAC from Direct Sales

**Risk:** $3,000 CAC too high, payback >6 months unsustainable

**Mitigation:**
- **Shift to lower-CAC channels faster:** Scale content, trade shows, referrals by Month 6 (not Month 9)
- **Improve sales efficiency:** Reduce sales cycle from 4 months → 3 months
- **Self-service trial:** Build product-led growth motion (free trial → paid without sales touch)
- **Upsell existing customers:** Expand from 8 users → 12 users (expansion revenue, $0 CAC)

**Monitoring:** Calculate CAC monthly - if >$2,500 in Month 6, accelerate channel diversification

---

### Risk 3: Supply Store Partnership Underperformance

**Risk:** Supply stores don't generate expected leads (target: 20 customers, actual: 5)

**Mitigation:**
- **Shift resources:** Reallocate budget to content marketing, trade shows
- **Diagnose issue:** Are contractors not attending demos? Is demo quality poor?
- **Pivot strategy:** Instead of in-store demos, do lunch-and-learns (higher commitment attendees)
- **Alternative partners:** Try smaller independent electrical suppliers (may be more motivated)

**Monitoring:** After 3 demo events, assess trial-start rate - if <30%, pivot strategy

---

### Risk 4: Product Adoption Resistance

**Risk:** Electricians don't adopt app despite pilot (senior electricians refuse)

**Mitigation:**
- **Identify resistance early:** Week 2 of pilot, survey electrician attitudes
- **Champion focus:** Lean on tech-savvy electricians to advocate
- **Gradual rollout:** Don't force full team adoption - start with 2-3, expand as they succeed
- **Voice input priority:** Ship voice-to-text feature ASAP (typing is #1 adoption barrier)
- **Offline mode guarantee:** Prove app works in basements, no-signal areas

**Monitoring:** Track weekly active users per pilot - if <50% by Week 8, extend pilot or redesign onboarding

---

### Risk 5: Competitor Entry

**Risk:** Existing construction software (ServiceTitan, Fieldwire) adds electrical-specific features

**Mitigation:**
- **Speed to market:** Get 100 customers ASAP (defensibility through network effects)
- **Deep vertical focus:** OCR + NEC validation + BC code = hard to replicate
- **Integration moat:** QuickBooks, PaddleOCR, electrical supplier integrations take time
- **Customer lock-in:** Once team is trained, switching cost is high
- **Community:** Build ECABC relationships, industry credibility

**Monitoring:** Track competitor feature releases, customer churn to competitors
