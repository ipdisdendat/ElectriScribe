# VALIDATION TASK 2: Business Requirements vs Industry Standards

## Your Role
You are a construction industry analyst who specializes in electrical contracting business operations, regulatory compliance, and technology adoption in the trades.

## What to Validate

### 1. Regulatory Compliance Requirements
**Claims about BC/Canadian electrical industry:**
- ESA (Electrical Safety Authority) inspection process and failure rates (15-20% claimed)
- WorkSafeBC documentation requirements
- BC Electrical Code (CEC 2021 + BC amendments) specific requirements
- AFCI/GFCI requirements mentioned
- ITA (Industry Training Authority) apprentice tracking

**Questions:**
- Are the regulatory references accurate for BC?
- Are the compliance pain points real for BC contractors?
- Are the inspection failure rates realistic?
- Are there missing regulatory requirements the app should address?

### 2. Trade Contractor Business Operations
**Claims about typical operations:**
- 500-800 service calls/year for 5-15 person company
- 3-5% warranty/callback rate
- Project mix: Residential, light commercial, service work
- Apprentice-to-journeyman ratios
- Team sizes and structures

**Questions:**
- Are these operational metrics realistic for mid-size electrical contractors?
- Is the service call volume appropriate for team size?
- Are the business pain points (callbacks, documentation, training) universal or overstated?

### 3. Technology Adoption in Trades
**Claims about current state:**
- Using QuickBooks Online for accounting (majority)
- Manual Excel/Word for quotes (common)
- Google Drive for photo storage (unorganized)
- Resistance to new technology from senior electricians
- Apprentices are "tech-native" (80%)

**Questions:**
- What's the actual technology adoption rate in electrical contracting?
- What competitive tools exist (ServiceTitan, Fieldwire, Jobber, etc.)?
- Are the adoption barriers realistic?
- What does successful rollout look like in trades businesses?

### 4. Insurance and Liability
**Claims:**
- E&O insurance: $8,000-12,000/year
- Potential 10-15% reduction with documented compliance
- Lawsuit costs: $50,000-500,000
- WorkSafeBC penalties: $5,000-50,000

**Questions:**
- Are these insurance costs typical for BC electrical contractors?
- Do insurance companies actually offer discounts for compliance documentation systems?
- Are the liability risk estimates realistic?

### 5. Training and Apprenticeship
**Claims:**
- 2-3 hours/day journeyman time spent training apprentices
- Red Seal apprenticeship program (4 years)
- Apprentice wages: 80-90% of journeyman rate
- App could reduce apprentice training time by 6 months

**Questions:**
- Is 2-3 hours/day training time realistic or overstated?
- What's the actual cost of apprentice training in the trades?
- Can technology realistically accelerate apprentice development?
- What are ITA requirements for apprentice hour logging?

### 6. Customer Expectations
**Claims:**
- High-end residential (West Van, Kits) expect professional presentation
- Same-day quotes increase win rate by 20-30%
- Professional reports justify 10-15% premium pricing
- Customers care about AI-powered documentation

**Questions:**
- What do residential/commercial customers actually value?
- Is same-day quoting truly a differentiator, or is price still primary?
- Do customers pay premiums for better documentation, or just lowest price?

### 7. Competitive Landscape
**Claims about Vancouver/Lower Mainland market:**
- Old-school contractors (clipboard, handwritten)
- High-end contractors (ServiceTitan, premium pricing)
- Middle ground opportunity (app enables premium service without premium costs)

**Questions:**
- Is this market segmentation accurate?
- What's the actual competitive landscape in Vancouver electrical contracting?
- Is there truly a "middle ground" opportunity, or is it a race to the bottom on price?

## Deliverable
Provide:
1. **VALIDATED** - Which industry claims are accurate
2. **QUESTIONABLE** - Which claims seem idealized or outdated
3. **MISSING** - What real industry pain points/requirements are not addressed
4. **COMPETITIVE REALITY** - What's the actual state of the market and technology adoption
