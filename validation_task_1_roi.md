# VALIDATION TASK 1: ROI Calculations & Pricing Reality Check

## Your Role
You are a B2B SaaS pricing analyst and business consultant who specializes in trade contractor software. Review the ROI calculations and pricing expectations in the draft business analysis.

## What to Validate

### 1. Labor Cost Calculations
**Claims to verify:**
- Journeyman billable rate: $85-95/hr in Vancouver/Lower Mainland
- Journeyman total cost (with burden): $52.50-62.40/hr
- Margin calculation: 38-45%
- Apprentice rates and margins

**Questions:**
- Are these rates realistic for BC electrical contractors in 2024?
- Is the burden percentage (25-30%) accurate for BC (WCB, CPP, EI, benefits)?
- Do the margins align with industry standards?

### 2. Time Savings Claims
**Claims to verify:**
- Panel documentation currently takes 30-45 minutes, app reduces to 10-20 minutes (20-30 min saved)
- Apprentice question time: 2-3 hours/day journeyman time spent training
- Quote preparation: 2-3 hours currently, 30 minutes with app

**Questions:**
- Are these baseline times realistic?
- Are the time savings achievable with current AI/OCR technology?
- Is the 55 minutes/day total savings per electrician realistic?

### 3. ROI Calculation Validity
**Claim:** $169,400/year productivity gain for 10 electricians, with $8,000/year investment = 2,018% ROI

**Questions:**
- Is the ROI calculation methodology sound?
- Are all cost factors included (training, adoption friction, ongoing support)?
- Is the "pays for itself in 2 weeks" claim defensible?
- What's a more conservative ROI estimate?

### 4. Callback Cost Estimates
**Claims:**
- Average callback cost: $500
- Reduce callbacks from 5% to 2% = 15 callbacks avoided/year = $7,500 savings
- Wire gauge error cost: $800-2,000 per incident

**Questions:**
- Are these callback cost estimates realistic?
- Is 5% callback rate normal for electrical contractors?
- Can an app realistically reduce callbacks from 5% to 2%?

### 5. Pricing Expectations
**Claims:**
- Willing to pay $35-45/user/month
- Company license: $500-750/month acceptable
- Break-even: App needs to save 20 minutes/electrician/week

**Questions:**
- How does this compare to comparable B2B SaaS tools (ServiceTitan, Fieldwire, Procore)?
- Is the pricing expectation realistic given the claimed value?
- Are there hidden costs not accounted for (data storage, API usage, support)?

### 6. Business Scenario Costs
**Scenario costs claimed:**
- Panel Upgrade Nightmare: $1,040 + reputation damage
- Wire Gauge Error: $170 + liability risk
- ESA Inspection Failure: $2,262
- Knowledge Loss: $125/incident

**Questions:**
- Are these scenario costs realistic?
- Are the frequencies realistic (e.g., 3-5 panel upgrade issues/year)?
- Are the prevention claims realistic (can app actually prevent these scenarios)?

## Deliverable
Provide:
1. **VALIDATED** - Which calculations are sound and realistic
2. **QUESTIONABLE** - Which calculations seem optimistic or need adjustment
3. **CORRECTED** - Provide more conservative/realistic numbers where needed
4. **OVERALL ROI ASSESSMENT** - What's a defensible, conservative ROI claim?
