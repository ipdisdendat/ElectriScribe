  - **Range: $400-700 per callback**

### Callback Rate (5% → 2% claimed)
- **Current 5% baseline:** ⚠️ SLIGHTLY HIGH
  - Industry average: 3-5% for service work, 5-8% for new construction
  - Well-managed contractors: 2-3%
  - **Adjustment:** Assume current baseline 4% (not 5%)

- **App-reduced to 2%:** ⚠️ OPTIMISTIC

  - App can't prevent installation errors, damaged materials, etc.
  - **Adjustment:** Assume reduction to 2.5% (not 2%)
  - **Realistic savings:** 4% → 2.5% = 1.5% of 500 jobs = 7.5 callbacks/year × $500 = $3,750/year (not $7,500)

### Specific Error Costs
- Wire gauge error $800-2,000: ✅ REALISTIC (depends on run length, accessibility)
- ESA inspection failure $2,262: ✅ REALISTIC (fee + delay penalties real on commercial jobs)

**VERDICT:** Callback savings real but overstated by 40-50%. Adjust to $3,750-5,000/year.
